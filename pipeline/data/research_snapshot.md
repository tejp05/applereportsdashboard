## 5. Credit ratings

Current (verified Aug 2026): **Moody's Aaa (stable)** | **S&P AA+ (stable)**. **S&P has NOT upgraded Apple to AAA** — it affirmed AA+ as recently as 2025-05-19; Apple is split-rated (Moody's-only Aaa). Any dashboard claim of an S&P AAA is wrong.

Rating-action history:
- **Late Apr 2013** — first-ever ratings, assigned ahead of the record $17B bond deal priced 2013-04-30: S&P **AA+** / Moody's **Aa1**, both stable. (Moody's explicitly kept Apple one notch below Aaa.)
- **2013–2021** — no upgrades/downgrades found; ratings held at AA+/Aa1 (interim outlook actions: null — none surfaced in sources).
- **2021-12-21** — Moody's upgrades senior unsecured **Aa1 → Aaa**, stable; P-1 commercial paper affirmed. Joined Microsoft and J&J as the only S&P 500 US corporates at top rating.
- **2024-05-17** — S&P affirms **AA+**, outlook stable.
- **2025-05-19** — S&P affirms **AA+**, outlook stable; new senior unsecured notes assigned AA+.
- **2022–2026** — no other rating changes found; Moody's Aaa carried into 2026 compilations. No 2026 rating action found by either agency (null).
